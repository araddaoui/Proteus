# Proteus AI Output & Decision-Making Disclaimer

**Operator:** Ali Raddaoui, doing business as "Proteus"
**Effective Date:** [EFFECTIVE DATE] · **Version:** 1.0

This is the most important document in this package. Read it before relying on anything Proteus produces.

## 1. Proteus Output Is Advisory

Every Scenario, Evidence Ledger claim, Stakeholder Impact analysis, Red-Flag match, and Recommended Action that Proteus generates is advisory input to a human decision process. **None of it is:**

- legal advice;
- accreditation or regulatory approval;
- financial or investment advice;
- HR advice;
- a student disciplinary determination;
- an admissions determination;
- a grading decision; or
- a tenure or promotion decision.

## 2. No Consequential Decision Should Rely Solely on Proteus

Decisions touching grading, enrollment, discipline, employment, or other consequential outcomes require independent human judgment and, where Proteus's governance gates apply, explicit sign-off from the appropriate institutional bodies (for example, both an academic and an administrative authority). A Proteus recommendation — including a "Do Not Adopt" or "Do Not Use AI" outcome — is itself advisory and does not substitute for your institution's actual governance process.

Do not use Proteus to make any decision that is required by law, regulation, accreditation standard, or institutional policy to be made by a qualified human, unless that human independently reviews and adopts the result.

## 3. AI Can Be Wrong

AI-generated content may be inaccurate, outdated, incomplete, or biased, and may reflect errors in its source material or training data. Proteus is designed to flag uncertainty and distinguish evidence from inference where possible, but it cannot guarantee the correctness of any output, including evidence summaries, scenario forecasts, or red-flag pattern matches.

## 4. Deterministic vs. AI-Assisted Modes

Proteus runs in **deterministic mode by default**. In this mode, scenario drafts are produced from Proteus's own built-in templates and rules; **no text is sent to any AI model provider** and no AI API key is required. Deterministic output is still advisory and subject to every limitation in this document.

**AI-assisted mode** is opt-in and active only when explicitly enabled server-side. In that mode, the text you submit for those specific functions is processed by a third-party model provider (currently Google Gemini when configured). AI providers may retain requests under their own terms, which we do not control. **Do not submit confidential, regulated, or personal data to AI features in preview/demo mode.**

## 5. Evidence Requires a Source, Not Guaranteed Accuracy

Proteus requires every Evidence Ledger entry to carry a source link before it can be saved. This enforces *traceability*, not *correctness* — Proteus does not independently verify that a cited source is accurate, current, or peer-reviewed. You are responsible for evaluating the quality of any source before relying on the claim it supports.

## 6. Customer Remains Responsible

Your institution remains fully responsible for any decision made using Proteus, regardless of what Proteus recommended, including decisions that follow or depart from a Proteus recommendation.

## 7. Assumption of Risk

You accept that AI output is probabilistic and may be wrong, and you assume the risk of relying on it.

## 8. Non-Adoption Is a Valid Outcome

Proteus is designed to be capable of concluding that AI is not an appropriate solution for a given use case. A "Do Not Adopt" or "Do Not Use AI" outcome is a legitimate result of the deliberation process, not a system malfunction, and should be given the same weight as any other recommendation.
