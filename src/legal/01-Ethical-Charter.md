# Proteus Ethical Charter

**Product:** Proteus — Institutional Intelligence, Foresight, Stewardship & Decision-Support for Higher Education
**Operated by:** Ali Raddaoui, doing business as "Proteus"
**Effective Date:** [EFFECTIVE DATE] · **Version:** 1.0

## 1. Purpose

Proteus exists to help universities understand themselves, understand change, explore possible futures, weigh alternatives, and make better-informed institutional decisions. **Proteus does not decide what a university should become.** Human beings and the institution's own governance retain final authority.

## 2. Operating Principles

- **Human primacy.** Proteus supports human judgment; it does not replace it.
- **Common good before optimization.** Efficiency, cost, novelty, or vendor interest never automatically outrank educational, civic, or human value.
- **Institutional autonomy.** Each customer institution keeps authority over its own mission, policy, and governance.
- **Academic freedom and pluralism.** Market demand or technological trend is never, by itself, sufficient reason to eliminate a discipline, program, or line of inquiry.
- **Transparency.** Wherever reasonably possible, Proteus distinguishes evidence, inference, assumption, uncertainty, recommendation, and institutional decision from one another, rather than presenting all five as equivalent.
- **Privacy and data sovereignty.** Institutional and personal information is treated as entrusted, not owned.
- **Equity and dignity.** Proteus considers differential effects on students, faculty, staff, and affected communities, not just institutional averages.
- **No technological determinism.** That AI *can* perform a task is never treated as sufficient reason that it *should*.
- **Reversibility.** Where reasonably possible, consequential AI-assisted changes permit review, correction, or rollback.
- **Humility.** Uncertain conclusions are presented as uncertain, not as settled fact.
- **No single optimization doctrine.** Proteus does not assume the ideal university is necessarily the most automated, least expensive, most profitable, or most data-driven one.
- **Human veto.** Authorized humans may reject, override, or disregard any Proteus output, always.
