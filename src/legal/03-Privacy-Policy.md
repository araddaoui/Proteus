# Proteus Privacy Policy

**Operator:** Ali Raddaoui, doing business as "Proteus"
**Privacy Contact:** araddaoui@gmail.com
**Effective Date:** [EFFECTIVE DATE] · **Version:** 1.0

This Policy explains how we handle personal information in connection with Proteus. It is written to describe what Proteus actually does today.

## 1. Plain-Language Summary

- In preview/demo mode, the data you enter is stored **in your own browser**, not in a persistent server-side customer database.
- We do **not** sell your information and we do **not** use it to build advertising profiles.
- We do **not** use confidential institutional data to train general-purpose AI models for third parties.
- We use **no advertising or tracking cookies**.
- Third parties involved: **Vercel** (hosting), **Google Firebase** (optional Google sign-in), and **Google Gemini** (AI features, only when configured).

## 2. Scope and Operator

This Policy covers personal information processed through Proteus, operated by Ali Raddaoui, doing business as "Proteus."

## 3. Information We Process

Depending on how you use Proteus, this may include:

- **Account/identity information:** name, email address, institutional role or title, and (for Google sign-in) the profile information Google returns.
- **Technical and log information:** IP address, device/browser type, timestamps, and error/diagnostic data processed by our hosting provider when a request reaches our servers.
- **Content you enter:** text and records you create in the product (for example, decision drafts, evidence claims, and similar working content). In preview/demo mode this is stored in your browser.

## 4. Where Your Data Lives

In preview/demo mode, your working data is stored in your browser's local storage on your device. It stays there until you clear it. Our server component does not maintain a persistent customer database in preview mode. If you clear your browser storage or use a different device/browser, the data will not be available.

## 5. Cookies and Local Storage

We do **not** use advertising, analytics, or cross-site tracking cookies. We use browser local storage for essential functionality (for example, remembering your demo session and locally saved work). Third-party sign-in and hosting providers may set their own technical cookies or storage necessary for those services to function.

## 6. AI Processing

When AI features are enabled and a model provider is configured (currently Google Gemini), the text you submit for those specific functions is sent to that provider to generate a response. We send only the information necessary to deliver that function, and we do not send confidential data to AI providers. If no AI provider is configured, Proteus uses built-in fallback content and does not send your text to an AI provider.

**Do not enter confidential, regulated, or personal data into preview/demo AI features.** AI providers process requests under their own terms, which we do not control.

## 7. Third-Party Service Providers

We use a small set of providers to run Proteus:

| Provider | Purpose | Notes |
|---|---|---|
| Vercel | Application hosting and request handling | Processes technical/request data to serve the app. |
| Google Firebase | Optional Google sign-in | Processes sign-in identity when you choose Google. |
| Google Gemini | AI features (if configured) | Processes the text you submit for AI functions. |

Each provider processes data under its own privacy terms. We do not describe any provider's training practices except as confirmed by that provider's own terms or our contract with them.

## 8. Why We Process Information

To provide and operate the service; to authenticate users; to maintain security and prevent abuse; to provide support; to maintain backups and reliability; and to comply with law. Where laws such as the GDPR apply, we rely on appropriate bases (for example, performance of a contract, legitimate interests, and consent where required).

## 9. What We Do Not Do

- We do not sell your data or personal information.
- We do not build advertising profiles of universities, faculty, staff, or students.
- We do not use confidential customer data to train general-purpose AI models for unrelated third parties unless you expressly authorize it.
- We do not expose one institution's confidential data, evidence, or decision records to another institution.

## 10. Security

We use administrative, technical, and organizational safeguards appropriate to the nature and risk of the information we process, and we update them as the platform matures. **No system can be guaranteed completely secure. Preview/demo identity flows are not secure authentication and should not be used with real or confidential data.**

## 11. International Processing

Our hosting and service providers may process information in countries other than your own. Where required, we rely on appropriate transfer mechanisms (such as standard contractual clauses) once applicable.

## 12. Retention

In preview/demo mode, data stored in your browser is retained until you clear it. Server-side request and diagnostic logs are retained only as long as needed for security, operations, and legal compliance, then deleted or anonymized. We will publish specific retention periods before onboarding real institutional data.

## 13. Your Rights

Where applicable law provides rights of access, correction, deletion, restriction, portability, or objection, you may exercise them by contacting araddaoui@gmail.com. We will respond within the time required by applicable law. You may also clear locally stored data at any time through your browser and any in-product controls.

## 14. Children

Proteus is built for higher-education institutional use and is intended for adults (18+). It is not directed to children, and we do not knowingly process children's personal data, unless a specific deployment is expressly configured and contracted for that purpose.

## 15. Education Records

If an institution uses Proteus with education records, additional obligations (for example, under FERPA in the United States) may apply. Any such deployment requires a separate written agreement defining the parties' roles and responsibilities. Until then, do not enter education records.

## 16. Changes to This Policy

We will communicate material changes by reasonable means before they take effect.
