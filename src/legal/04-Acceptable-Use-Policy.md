## 2. Prohibited Uses

You agree **not** to:

- Use Proteus to conduct unlawful surveillance of students, faculty, or staff.
- Make or implement a prohibited automated decision — including any decision about admissions, grading, discipline, or employment — without the human review that Proteus's Decision Record and sign-off gates are designed to require.
- Use an assumed identity (including a demo persona) to **deceive or harm others**, to **gain access to another institution's real data**, or to **make or attribute decisions on someone else's behalf**. (Using the sanctioned preview selector for its intended demonstration purpose is not a violation of this clause.)
- Impersonate a real, identifiable person or institution in a way that misleads a third party.
- Upload information you are not authorized to share.
- Circumvent, disable, or attempt to bypass security or access controls.
- Introduce malware or attempt to gain unauthorized access to any part of the platform or another institution's data.
- Scrape, harvest, resell, sublicense, or benchmark Proteus or its outputs for a competing product without our written consent.
- Reverse-engineer protected components of Proteus except where expressly permitted by law.
- Treat Proteus's AI output as the sole basis for a consequential institutional decision, or present unverified AI output to others as verified fact.
- Harass or retaliate against any student, faculty member, staff member, or other individual using information obtained through Proteus.
- Covertly monitor employee productivity in a manner that violates applicable law or your institution's own policies.
- Use Proteus to evade your institution's legitimate governance processes — including by treating a Proteus recommendation as a substitute for required Faculty Senate, Provost, or Board approval.
